# Evidence-backed research relationships

Identify the central question and stable references for nodes. Use paper_lookup to resolve papers and paper_neighbors to verify citation direction when relevant. Distinguish a cited-by link, a methodological dependency, topical similarity and evidence supporting a claim. Treat inferred relationships as pending review. When assigning saved records to a graph, reuse their IDs and keep the source behind each relationship.

In a graph or star, use parentId for a genuine branch, dependsOn for a methodological prerequisite, and relatedIds for a non-directional association. In a lineage, use observed publication or event dates and link only documented relationships. On a bus, connect shared source or method cards to the groups they actually inform. Record the nature, evidence and verification status of each research relationship in the relevant card detail.
